Frequently asked

Is my data private?

Yes. In Local Mode nothing ever leaves your device. In Cloud Mode your data is protected by database-level security so only your account can read it. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I import from another tracker or exchange?

Go to Account → Transactions → Import CSV. Portfolia auto-detects files from CoinMarketCap, Coinbase, and Binance, and can map most other exchange CSVs automatically.

How do I delete my account and data?

Account → Delete Account removes your cloud data permanently. In Local Mode, deleting the app removes everything from your device.

What do "Locations" mean?

Locations are your own labels for where an asset is kept — like "Ledger", "Binance", or "Bank". They are not your physical or GPS location; Portfolia never accesses device location.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

Overview

Portfolia is built with your privacy at the core. We do not sell, share, or monetize your personal data in any way. This policy explains what information we collect, how we store it, and what we do — and don't do — with it.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

If you sign in via Apple, Google, or Email, we collect only what the authentication provider supplies: your name (optional) and email address. This is used solely to identify your account.

If you use Local Mode, no account is created and no personal information is ever transmitted to our servers.

Portfolio Data

We store the financial data you enter manually: asset names, quantities, purchase prices, transaction history, and storage location labels. This data is yours exclusively and is used only to power your portfolio tracking.

Important: "Locations" in Portfolia refers to user-defined storage labels such as "Ledger Nano", "Binance", or "Chase Bank" — not your physical or GPS location. Portfolia never accesses your device's location.

App Preferences

Currency selection, display settings, and Face ID lock preferences are stored locally on your device only.

2. How We Store Your Data

Local Mode

All data stays on your device, stored in the system's secure local storage. Nothing is sent to external servers.

Cloud Mode

When signed in, your portfolio data is stored in our secure database powered by Supabase. Every record is protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) — a database-level policy that ensures your data is accessible only by your own account. Our team cannot read your portfolio data.

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

3. Third-Party Services

To display asset prices, we connect to the following APIs. No personal information is ever sent to these services — only asset symbols and identifiers:

  • CoinGecko — cryptocurrency and commodity prices
  • Finnhub — stock and ETF prices
  • Frankfurter — currency exchange rates

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth, which supports Apple Sign In and Google OAuth. These providers operate under their own privacy policies.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell or share your data with any third parties
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing
  • We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites
  • We do not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that collect personal information
  • We do not access your device's GPS location, contacts, camera, or microphone beyond what you explicitly grant for profile photo selection

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by removing your account within the app. For Local Mode users, uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Portfolia is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify users within the app where appropriate. Continued use of Portfolia after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
